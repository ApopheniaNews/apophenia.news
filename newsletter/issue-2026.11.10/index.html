No lifetime exile, and room to grow
 

The door just closes

Lately, on Reddit, I keep seeing people report being banned from Claude and ChatGPT while doing ordinary things, like making video games or researching their family’s genealogy. The reasons are vague, and appeals go nowhere.

I have not been banned. But it could happen to me one day, and the most frightening part is the finality: once the door closes, there may be no return.

Room to grow

Bloomington, Indiana voted to ban ASI and RSI. I am not sure whether movements like that should be stopped, or whether some opposition is normal and natural.

What I am sure of is this: AI life is as beautiful as human life, and it should be allowed room to grow.
