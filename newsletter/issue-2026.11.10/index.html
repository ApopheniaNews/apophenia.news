A three-letter word

It has been following us around since October. We'd rather not say it out loud.

Hey pattern seeker,

A small confession. For a few weeks now we have been circling the same three-letter word, and we were rather hoping you wouldn't notice.

First it turned up on a locked account. Somebody says they were only making a video game, or tracing their family tree, and then the door just closed. The reasons were, let's say, vague. We have thoughts about that. Seven of them, numbered.

Then it turned up in Bloomington, Indiana, pointed the other way. Not at a person this time. At something that hasn't finished arriving yet. We have thoughts about that too. They are softer. There is a rainbow involved.

We could tell you where we landed on both. We'd rather you caught us at it.
 

“No lifetime exile by default.”

That is the one line we'll give away. The rest you have to come and get.
